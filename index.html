Welcome to ITC4214!

The course exposes students to today’s web development methodologies and programming principles. It provides students with the opportunity to develop complex, data driven web applications, enhancing their understanding of web development and their judgement of the effectiveness of different development techniques. Web application security concepts are also implemented. The course instructor is Dr. Leonardos Mageiros, and his office hours are after class or after booking an appointment on Teams.
